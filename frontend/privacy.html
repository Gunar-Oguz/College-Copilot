Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version: We collect only what we need to run CollegeCopilot. We never sell your data. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address. If you sign in with Google, we also receive your name and profile picture from Google.
  • What you save: your saved schools, private notes and reviews.
  • Search questions: the questions you ask, the answers we give and how long they took. These are stored without your account or email, so they aren't linked to you. Please don't type personal details (like your name, address or test scores) into questions.
  • Feedback: your 👍 / 👎 on answers, saved with the question and answer, also without your account.

We don't use analytics or advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To run your account and show your saved schools, notes and reviews.
  • To improve CollegeCopilot, for example by finding questions it answers badly and fixing them.
  • To keep the service safe and prevent abuse.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data to anyone.
  • We don't share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't show your name or email with your reviews.

Cookies and browser storage

CollegeCopilot doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. To keep you signed in, our sign-in provider (Supabase) stores your session in your browser's local storage. Signing out removes it.

Services we rely on

These companies process data for us only to provide the service:

  • Supabase: sign-in and account management (stores your email and login details).
  • Render: hosts the website and database.
  • Anthropic: the AI that reads your question and writes answers. Your question text is sent to Anthropic to generate the answer; your account details are not.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google."
  • Wikimedia Commons and jsDelivr: your browser loads campus photos and our sign-in code from their servers, so they see your IP address, like any website you visit.

How long we keep it

  • Account data (email, saved schools, notes, reviews) is kept until you ask us to delete it.
  • Search questions and feedback, which aren't linked to your account, are kept to improve the service.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete your account data at any time. Email us from the address on your account and we'll take care of it. Deleting your account removes your email, saved schools, notes and reviews.

Children

CollegeCopilot isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll let account holders know.

Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: support@collegecopilot.com.
