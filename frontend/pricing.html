Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from this page or the billing portal. You keep Pro until the end of the month you paid for, then move back to Free. Your saved schools stay, but you'll only be able to add more while you have fewer than 3.

When do free questions reset?

Every day at midnight UTC (7 or 8 pm Eastern, depending on daylight saving time).

What does "priority response queue" mean?

When many people are asking questions at once, Pro questions are answered first.

Is my payment information safe?

Payments are handled by Stripe. CollegeCopilot never sees or stores your card number.

Where does the data come from?

Official public sources such as the U.S. Department of Education's College Scorecard and IPEDS, the Bureau of Labor Statistics and Opportunity Insights. Always confirm important details with each school.
